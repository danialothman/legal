**Listening History and Community Totals**

The app keeps a record of your listening — which station, for how long, and when — in a database on your device. This powers the listening stats screen. It is never uploaded, and I cannot see it.

The app's settings include an option called **"Contribute to community totals"**. It is **off by default**, and nothing is uploaded unless you turn it on.

If you do turn it on, the app sends the following for each listening session, and nothing else:

- which station was played, as a number;
- how many milliseconds it was played for;
- the date it happened, as a calendar day such as `2026-07-27`;
- a random identifier generated for that one listening session.

What is deliberately **not** sent:

- any identifier for you, your device, or your installation of the app;
- the time of day you listened — only the date;
- anything that would allow two contributions to be recognised as coming from the same person.

The session identifier is a random value created for a single session. It exists so that if the app has to retry an upload, the same session is not counted twice. Because a new one is generated every time, it cannot be used to group your sessions together or to recognise you across sessions.

The result is that contributions arrive as unlinked, anonymous rows. They are used to understand how much each station is listened to overall. I cannot tell which contributions came from you, and neither can anyone else with access to the data.

Contributions are stored in a database hosted by Supabase, acting as a service provider on my behalf. You can switch the option off at any time to stop contributing.

**Backups**

If you have Android's backup feature enabled, Android may include your on-device listening history in the backup it makes to your own Google account. This is handled by Android and Google, not by me — I have no access to it. It is restored to your device if you reinstall the app. Your app settings, including the "Contribute to community totals" option, are deliberately excluded from this backup, so reinstalling the app always starts with contributions switched off.

**Data Retention and Deletion**

Your listening history is stored on your device and stays there until you delete it. You can clear it at any time using "Reset stats" in the app's listening stats screen, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Contributions to the community totals are, by design, anonymous and not linked to you. Because of this, once a contribution has been sent there is no way for me to identify which contributions came from any particular person, and therefore no way to retrieve or delete an individual's contributions on request. Switching off "Contribute to community totals" stops any further contributions being sent; it cannot withdraw ones already sent, because they cannot be picked out.
